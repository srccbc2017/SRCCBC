# SRCC Business Club (SRCCBC) — Official

Official website source for **SRCC Business Club**, a student business club at **Shaheed Ramiz Uddin Cantonment College**, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Founded in 2017.

🔗 **Live site:** https://srccbc2017.github.io/SRCCBC/
📘 **Facebook:** https://www.facebook.com/srccbc

## About

SRCC Business Club is dedicated to developing well-rounded, career-ready professionals. Through workshops, competitions, and hands-on projects, the club helps students build essential skills in leadership, entrepreneurship, public speaking, teamwork, and business strategy — bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world professional readiness.

## Note on naming

"SRCC" is shared by multiple unrelated entities — Shri Ram College of Commerce (Delhi, India) and Shaheed Ramiz Uddin Cantonment College (Dhaka, Bangladesh) both use this acronym. This repository and site refer specifically to the **SRCC Business Club at Shaheed Ramiz Uddin Cantonment College, Dhaka**.
